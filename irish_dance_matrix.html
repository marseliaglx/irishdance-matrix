Injury Prevention 🩹
Based on peer-reviewed research — Journal of Dance Medicine & Science, PMC systematic review of Irish dancing injuries
📊 What the research shows: A 2023 systematic review of Irish dance injuries found stress fractures are the most prevalent diagnosis (29.9% of clinical cases), followed by patellofemoral pain syndrome (11.1%), ankle sprains (5.1%), and plantar fasciitis (4.6%). 79.6% of injuries are overuse in nature — meaning they are largely preventable with smart training. 58% of injuries occur before major competitions, exactly when training load is highest.
